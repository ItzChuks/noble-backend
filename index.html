How it works

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Sign up with your name, email, country and phone number, and accept the terms. Then pay the one-time ₦5,000 t-shirt fee to become a registered member.

  2. 2

    Join your plans

    Pick one or several cash, grocery or foodie plans. Each plan has a ₦3,000 joining fee.

  3. 3

    Pay your contributions

    Pay securely online on Flutterwave. Payments are confirmed automatically.

  4. 4

    Collect your payout

    Request your payout when the plan is complete. Each payout request has a ₦3,000 clearance fee.

Fees and rules

Please read these before you join. You will be asked to accept them when you create your account.

👕

T-shirt registration

₦5,000

A compulsory one-time payment. Until it is paid you are an unregistered member and cannot join a plan, make a payment or request a payout.

🎟️

Joining fee

₦3,000

Paid before you join any plan, each time you join one. You join as soon as the payment is confirmed.

✅

Payout clearance fee

₦3,000

Paid with every payout request. Your request is sent as soon as the fee is confirmed.

  • Each plan shows its exact number of payments (for example 12 or 13 weeks, or 50 or 52 weeks). You pay for exactly the number of payments shown on the plan you join.
  • Daily plans are due before 12:00 noon each day, weekly plans by Friday 12:00 noon, and monthly plans by the 2nd of the following month (Lagos time). A late payment costs double, and the penalty does not count towards your savings.
  • A payout can be requested only when your plan is fully paid and its saving period has ended.

Questions, answered

How long does a plan run?

Every plan shows its own number of payments. For example a weekly plan can run 12 or 13 weeks, or 50 or 52 weeks, and you pay for exactly the number shown on the plan.

Can I join more than one plan?

Yes. Run as many plans as you like at the same time. A ₦3,000 joining fee is paid each time you join a plan.

How do I pay?

Pay securely on Flutterwave from your dashboard. Payments are confirmed automatically. Before anything else you pay the one-time ₦5,000 t-shirt registration fee.

When can I collect my payout?

When your confirmed payments reach 100% of the plan and its saving period has ended, request it from My contributions. A ₦3,000 clearance fee applies to every payout request.
